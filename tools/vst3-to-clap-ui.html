VST3 → CLAP

Make a portable CLAP package. Everything runs locally in your browser; your plug-in files are not uploaded anywhere.

Choose the matching OS and CPU architecture. For macOS, choose the vst3-to-clap-wrapper.clap folder. Folder selection is supported in Chrome, Edge, and Safari.

Choose the .vst3 folder itself. The output ZIP includes a copy so its CLAP can find it.

Download CLAP package
Install: extract the downloaded ZIP into your host's CLAP plug-in folder. Keep the .clap file/bundle and adjacent .vst3 folder together. The wrapper must match your OS and CPU architecture. On macOS, patching invalidates the bundle signature; the UI displays the command to ad-hoc sign the bundle in Terminal after extraction.
